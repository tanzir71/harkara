# Before a flood

**Call 999 first if** water is rising fast and anyone is trapped, cut off or in danger.

Floods in Bangladesh come from monsoon rain, rivers overflowing and, in Dhaka, blocked drains. River floods usually give some warning. Use that time.

## Know the warnings

- The Flood Forecasting and Warning Centre (FFWC) of the Bangladesh Water Development Board forecasts river levels and says when rivers will cross the "danger level".
- The Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) warns of heavy rain.
- Call **1090** (free) for recorded weather and flood warnings.
- Listen to the radio, TV and announcements from your ward, union or city corporation.
- In Harkara, Alert posts in Nearby show what neighbours are reporting.

## Know your risk

- Is your home low-lying, near a river, canal or khal, or in a street that waterlogs?
- How high did the water come last time? Mark it on a wall.
- Where is the nearest high ground, flood shelter, school or tall concrete building?
- Which route will stay open when roads flood?

## Prepare the home

1. Raise beds, furniture, electrical goods and food stores on bricks or platforms.
2. Keep important documents in waterproof bags, high up.
3. Learn how to switch off electricity at the main switch and gas at the valve.
4. Store clean drinking water in covered containers. Raise the tubewell platform if you can.
5. Keep dry food: chira, muri, biscuits, gur, dates, powdered milk.
6. Plan where livestock and poultry will go, and store fodder high.
7. Clear rubbish from drains around your home.

## Prepare the family

- Pack a go-bag with water purification tablets, ORS, medicines, a torch and a power bank. See "Go-bag and family plan".
- Agree where to meet if you are separated, and who will help children, older people and anyone with a disability.
- Teach children to keep away from flood water.
- Charge phones and power banks before the water comes. Set Harkara mesh to Saver to make the battery last.
- Add neighbours as Harkara contacts, so you can reach each other over the mesh if the network goes down.

## When a warning comes

1. Move valuables and food higher.
2. Send children, older people, pregnant women and people with disabilities to safety early.
3. If told to evacuate, go. Do not wait for water to enter the house.
4. Switch off electricity and gas, lock up and take your go-bag.
