# During an earthquake

**Call 999 first** once the shaking stops, if someone is trapped or injured, or there is a fire or gas leak.

Dhaka faces a serious earthquake risk. Several active faults lie within reach of the city, much of it is built on filled land and soft soil that shakes more, and many buildings were not built to the national building code. Knowing what to do in the first seconds saves lives.

## Drop, Cover, Hold on

When you feel shaking:

1. **Drop** onto your hands and knees.
2. **Cover** your head and neck. Get under a sturdy table or desk if one is close. If not, crouch next to an inside wall, away from windows, and cover your head and neck with your arms.
3. **Hold on** to your shelter until the shaking stops. Move with it if it shifts.

Most shaking lasts less than a minute.

## Indoors

- **Stay inside until the shaking stops.** Many injuries happen when people run out and are hit by falling glass, plaster, railings or signboards.
- Keep away from windows, glass, mirrors, shelves, almirahs and hanging lights or fans.
- **Do not use the lift.**
- **Do not rush down the stairs while it is shaking.** Stairways get crowded and can break.
- **Do not jump from windows or balconies.** In past tremors in and around Dhaka, people were badly hurt by jumping and in stair crushes.
- In bed: stay there, lie face down and protect your head with a pillow.
- In a wheelchair: lock the wheels, bend forward and cover your head and neck.
- In the kitchen: move away from the stove. Turn it off only if you can do it at once.

## Outdoors

- Move to an open space, away from buildings, walls, signboards, electric poles, wires and trees.
- Drop down and cover your head until the shaking stops.
- In a narrow lane where you cannot reach open space, protect your head and keep away from old walls, balconies and overhanging signs.

## In a vehicle

- Stop in a clear place, away from bridges, flyovers, buildings, trees and wires.
- Stay inside with your seatbelt on until the shaking stops.
- Then drive slowly. Avoid bridges and flyovers that may be damaged.

## In crowded places

In malls, cinemas, mosques, classrooms and factory floors, do not rush for the exit. Drop, cover and hold on where you are. Leave calmly by the stairs after the shaking stops.

## Prepare now

- Fix almirahs, shelves, water heaters, TVs and gas cylinders to the wall.
- Keep heavy things on low shelves.
- Know the safe spots in every room and the nearest open space outside.
- Keep shoes and a torch next to your bed.
- Ask the owner whether the building was designed by an engineer and follows the Bangladesh National Building Code. Report cracks in columns and beams.
- Practise drop, cover, hold on at home, school and work.
