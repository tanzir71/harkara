# Allergic reactions and anaphylaxis

**Call 999 first if** there is any difficulty breathing, swelling of the face, lips, tongue or throat, wheezing, a hoarse voice, fainting or collapse after a food, sting, bite or medicine.

## Mild allergic reaction

Signs: itchy rash or hives, sneezing, runny nose, itchy eyes, mild swelling of the lips only.

1. Remove or avoid the cause.
2. An antihistamine tablet can help.
3. Watch closely for at least an hour. A mild reaction can get worse.

## Anaphylaxis: severe allergic reaction

It can start within minutes. Common causes: nuts, prawns and other shellfish, eggs, milk, bee and wasp stings, some medicines and injections.

Signs, any of:

- **Airway:** swollen tongue or throat, hoarse voice, trouble swallowing.
- **Breathing:** wheezing, noisy or difficult breathing, a cough that will not stop.
- **Circulation:** pale, clammy skin, dizziness, fainting, collapse.
- Often with rash, hives, swelling, vomiting or stomach pain.

## What to do

1. Call 999 and say "anaphylaxis".
2. If the person has an **adrenaline (epinephrine) auto-injector**, help them use it at once: into the outer middle of the thigh, through clothing if needed. Hold it in place for the time written on the device.
3. Position:
   - Trouble breathing: let them sit up.
   - Faint or dizzy: lay them flat with legs raised.
   - Pregnant: lay her on her left side.
   - **Do not let them stand or walk**, even if they feel better.
4. If there is no improvement after 5 minutes and there is a second auto-injector, use it.
5. If they become unresponsive and stop breathing normally, start CPR.
6. They must go to hospital even if they recover. Symptoms can come back hours later.

Auto-injectors are hard to find in Bangladesh. Without one, getting to a hospital emergency department quickly matters most: doctors there can give adrenaline.

## Insect stings

- Scrape a bee sting out sideways with a fingernail or card. Do not squeeze it.
- Wash the area and put on a cold pack.
- Watch for signs of anaphylaxis for at least 30 minutes.

## If you have a known severe allergy

- Carry your prescribed medicines at all times and keep them in date.
- Tell family, friends, teachers and colleagues what to do.
- Ask about ingredients when eating out or buying street food.
