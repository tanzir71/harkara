# CPR for adults

**Call 999 first if** an adult is unresponsive and not breathing normally. Put the phone on speaker and start CPR at once.

CPR keeps blood flowing to the brain until help comes. You cannot make things worse: a person who is not breathing is already dying.

## Chest compressions

1. Lay the person on their back on a firm surface: the floor, not a bed.
2. Kneel beside their chest.
3. Put the heel of one hand in the centre of the chest, on the lower half of the breastbone. Put your other hand on top and lock your fingers.
4. Keep your arms straight, shoulders directly above your hands.
5. Push straight down **5 to 6 cm**.
6. Let the chest come all the way back up after each push.
7. Push at **100 to 120 a minute**, about two pushes a second.

## Rescue breaths (if trained and willing)

1. After 30 compressions, open the airway: tilt the head back, lift the chin.
2. Pinch the nose. Seal your mouth over theirs.
3. Blow steadily for about 1 second until the chest rises.
4. Give 2 breaths, then go straight back to 30 compressions.

If you are not trained, or do not want to give breaths, do **compression-only CPR**: push hard and fast without stopping.

## Defibrillator (AED)

1. If someone brings an AED, switch it on and follow the voice.
2. Stick the pads on the bare chest as shown on the pads.
3. When it says to stand clear, make sure nobody touches the person.
4. After a shock, or if no shock is advised, restart CPR at once.

## Keep going until

- Help arrives and takes over, or
- The person starts breathing normally, coughs, moves or opens their eyes, or
- You are too exhausted, or it becomes unsafe.

If someone else knows CPR, swap every 2 minutes with almost no pause. If the person starts breathing, put them in the recovery position and watch closely.

## Pregnant woman

Do CPR as normal. If someone is with you, ask them to push the bump gently towards her left side with their hands while you push on the chest.

## Do not

- **Do not stop to feel for a pulse.** Watch for normal breathing instead.
- **Do not do CPR on a soft bed.** Move the person to the floor.
- **Do not lean on the chest** between pushes.
- Do not stop because ribs crack. It can happen; keep going.
