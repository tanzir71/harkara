# Eye injuries

**Call 999 or go to hospital at once if** an object is stuck in the eye, the eye or eyelid is cut, a chemical has splashed in, sight is lost or blurred, or the eye was hit hard.

## Chemical in the eye

Acid, alkali, lime (chun), cement, bleach or cleaning products.

1. **Rinse at once** with clean running water for at least **15 to 20 minutes**. Do not wait to find special drops.
2. Hold the eyelids open. Let the water run from the inner corner (by the nose) to the outer corner, so it does not wash into the other eye.
3. Remove contact lenses if they come out easily.
4. Go to hospital and keep rinsing on the way if you can. Take the chemical's name or container.

**Lime and cement can blind. Rinse for longer.**

## Dust or grit

1. Do not rub the eye.
2. Blink several times and let tears wash it out.
3. Rinse with clean water from a cup or bottle.
4. If you can see it on the white of the eye or inside the lower lid, lift it off with the wet corner of a clean cloth.
5. See a doctor if it will not come out, or the eye stays red and painful.

## Object stuck in the eye

1. **Do not pull it out, and do not press on the eye.**
2. Cover the eye with a paper cup or a ring of cloth so nothing touches the object.
3. Cover the other eye lightly too, because both eyes move together. Reassure the person.
4. Keep them still and go to hospital.

## Blow to the eye

Hold a cold pack wrapped in cloth gently on the area for 15 minutes, without pressing on the eye. See a doctor for pain, blurred or double vision, blood in the coloured part of the eye, or flashes of light.

## Cut to the eye or eyelid

Cover gently with a clean pad without pressing, and go to hospital.

In Dhaka, the National Institute of Ophthalmology and Hospital in Sher-e-Bangla Nagar treats eye emergencies.

## Do not

- **Do not rub the eye.**
- **Do not put breast milk, honey, oil, herbs or home-made drops in the eye.**
- **Do not use steroid eye drops** unless a doctor prescribes them.
