# Dog bites, animal bites and rabies

**Go to a hospital the same day** after any bite or scratch from a dog, cat, monkey, jackal, mongoose or bat that breaks the skin. **Call 999 first if** bleeding is heavy or the bite is severe, especially on a child's face, head or neck.

Once symptoms start, rabies almost always kills. It can be fully prevented by washing the wound and getting the vaccine in time.

## Wash the wound now

1. Wash the bite or scratch with soap and plenty of running water for **15 minutes**. Use a tap, jug or bottle and keep pouring.
2. If you have it, put on an antiseptic such as povidone-iodine after washing.
3. Cover loosely with a clean cloth.

Washing removes much of the virus. Do it before you leave for hospital.

## Get the vaccine

1. Go the same day to a government hospital, upazila health complex or rabies prevention centre. In Dhaka, the Infectious Diseases Hospital in Mohakhali has long treated animal bites. Bangladesh's national rabies programme supplies the vaccine to government facilities.
2. You will get anti-rabies vaccine (ARV) in several doses over a few weeks. For deep or many bites, or bites on the head and neck, you may also need rabies immunoglobulin.
3. **Complete every dose on the dates you are given.** Write them down.
4. If one place has no vaccine, go to another the same day. Do not wait.
5. Ask about a tetanus injection too.

## Who needs the vaccine

- Any bite or scratch that breaks the skin or draws blood.
- A lick on broken skin or a wound, or saliva in the eyes, nose or mouth.
- Any contact with a bat.

A lick on unbroken skin, or touching or feeding an animal, does not need the vaccine.

## Do not

- **Do not put chilli, lime, turmeric, mustard oil, kerosene, mud or toothpaste on the wound.**
- **Do not have the wound stitched closed** straight away, unless a doctor decides to.
- **Do not wait to see if the dog falls ill.** Start the vaccine now.
- Traditional and spiritual treatments may comfort, but **only the vaccine protects against rabies.**

## Watch the animal

If a dog or cat can be watched safely, observe it for 10 days. If it dies, disappears or acts strangely, tell your doctor. Do not try to catch a dog that is acting strangely.

## Prevent bites

- Leave dogs alone when they are eating, sleeping or with puppies.
- Teach children not to tease dogs or run from them: stand still, look away, back off slowly.
- Vaccinate pet dogs and cats every year, and support mass dog vaccination in your area.
