# Waterlogged streets in Dhaka

**Call 999 first if** someone falls into a drain or manhole, is swept away, or touches a live wire in the water.

After heavy rain, many Dhaka streets flood knee-deep or more for hours. The water hides open drains, missing manhole covers, broken roads and live electric wires. People have died in waterlogged streets from electric shock and from falling into drains.

## Before you go out

- Check Harkara Nearby for Traffic and Alert posts about flooded roads, and post what you see.
- If you can, wait for the water to go down. Most streets drain within hours.
- Wear closed shoes that grip, to protect your feet from glass and metal.
- Put your phone, wallet and papers in a plastic bag.

## Walking through water

1. Follow a path others are using safely, away from the road edge, where drains and footpath gaps are.
2. Probe ahead with a stick or umbrella.
3. Watch for swirling water or small whirlpools. They mean a drain or open manhole underneath. **Keep away.**
4. Hold children's hands firmly, or carry small children.
5. Walk slowly. Do not run.

## Live wires

- **Keep away from electric poles, lamp posts, transformers, meter boxes, hanging wires and lit signboards** in or near the water.
- If you see sparks or feel tingling in the water, go back the way you came, with small steps.
- **Do not touch someone who has been shocked while they are still in the water.** Shout for the power to be cut and call 999.
- Report fallen or hanging wires to 999 and to your electricity provider.

## Vehicles

- Do not drive or ride into water if you cannot see the road under it.
- If an engine stalls in water, do not restart it. Push the vehicle to higher ground or leave it.
- Rickshaws and CNGs can tip in hidden potholes. Hold on, and be ready to step off.

## After the water goes

- Wash your feet and legs with soap and clean water, and dry well between the toes.
- Clean and cover any cut. See a doctor if it gets red, swollen or you get a fever.
- Fever with muscle pain a week or two after wading may be leptospirosis. See a doctor.

## Neighbourhood action

- Keep drains clear: do not throw polythene, bottles or food waste into them.
- Mark open manholes with a stick, branch or red cloth, and report them to the city corporation.
- Share dangerous spots on Harkara Nearby so others can avoid them.
