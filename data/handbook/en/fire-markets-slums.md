# Fire in markets, crowded areas and chemical stores

**Call 999 first.** In crowded places fire spreads very fast. Get out, and get others out.

Dhaka has lost many lives to fires in crowded markets, narrow lanes, informal settlements (bostis) and buildings where chemicals were stored under homes, especially in Old Dhaka. Fire engines can take time to get through narrow lanes, so the first minutes depend on the people who are there.

## In a market or shopping centre

1. Raise the alarm loudly. Warn shopkeepers and customers.
2. Leave by the nearest exit. When you enter a market, notice two ways out.
3. Walk fast but do not run or push. Help children and older people.
4. **Do not go back for goods or cash. Your life is worth more than your shop.**
5. Once out, move well away to an open place, and keep the road clear for fire engines.
6. Shopkeepers: switch off the main power on the way out, if it is safe.

## In narrow lanes and bostis

1. Wake everyone: shout, and bang on doors and tin walls.
2. Carry out children, older people and anyone sick or disabled.
3. Move away from the fire, upwind, and away from gas pipes and cylinders.
4. Turn off the gas and electricity mains only if you can do it safely.
5. Clear the way for fire hoses, and show firefighters the nearest water: ponds, tanks, reservoirs.
6. A bucket line from a pond can help with a small fire **only if it is safe.** Stop and leave if it grows.

## Chemical warehouses and factories

Some buildings store chemicals, plastics, perfume, lighter gas and other flammable goods, sometimes under homes and shops.

- **Do not try to fight a chemical fire.** Water can spread it or cause explosions.
- **Evacuate at once and move far away**, several buildings away and upwind.
- Explosions, toxic smoke and burning liquid can spread suddenly.
- Tell 999 that chemicals are stored there, and what kind if you know.
- If you breathed the smoke and have red eyes, cough, headache or breathlessness, see a doctor.

## Onlookers make fires deadlier

- Crowds block fire engines, ambulances and escape routes.
- **Do not gather to watch or film.** Clear the road.
- Help by keeping the way open, guiding the fire service in, and caring for injured people away from the fire.

## Prevent fires in markets and shared buildings

- Do not store chemicals, gas cylinders or fuel in homes, shops or stairwells.
- Keep exits, stairs and lanes free of goods and stalls.
- Get wiring checked, avoid tangled and overloaded wires, and switch off power at closing time.
- Keep an extinguisher or sand bucket in each shop, and learn how to use it.
- Agree a meeting point, and a night guard who can raise the alarm.
- Do not leave mosquito coils, candles or stoves burning unattended near cloth or goods.
