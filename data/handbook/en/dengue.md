# Dengue

**Go to hospital at once, or call 999, if** a person with dengue shows any warning sign below, especially as the fever goes down.

Dengue is a virus spread by Aedes mosquitoes, which bite mostly in the daytime. Dhaka has large outbreaks, mostly from June to November. Most people recover at home, but some get severe dengue, which can kill.

## Common symptoms

- Sudden high fever for 2 to 7 days.
- Severe headache and pain behind the eyes.
- Muscle, joint and bone pain.
- Nausea, vomiting, rash.

With fever in the dengue season, see a doctor and get a dengue test (NS1 in the first days) at a hospital or a reliable lab.

## Warning signs: go to hospital now

They often appear **when the fever goes down**, around day 3 to 7. This is the most dangerous time.

- Severe stomach pain or tenderness.
- Vomiting again and again, or unable to drink.
- Bleeding from the gums or nose, blood in vomit or stool, black stool, heavy periods.
- Fast or difficult breathing.
- Great tiredness, restlessness, irritability or confusion.
- Cold, clammy hands and feet.
- Little or no urine for 6 hours or more.
- Swollen belly, or fainting.

Pregnant women, babies, older people and people with diabetes, high blood pressure or kidney disease should see a doctor early.

## Care at home (if there are no warning signs)

1. Rest.
2. Drink often through the day: water, ORS, soup, coconut water, fresh juice, rice water.
3. Check urine: it should be light yellow and regular.
4. **For fever and pain, use paracetamol only.** Adults: 500 mg to 1 g at a time, at least 6 hours apart, no more than 4 g (eight 500 mg tablets) in 24 hours. Children: dose by weight, as on the label or as a doctor says.
5. Sponge with lukewarm water to ease fever.
6. Sleep under a mosquito net, even in the daytime, so mosquitoes cannot pass the virus on.
7. Go back for blood tests (platelets and haematocrit) when the doctor asks.

## Do not

- **Do not take ibuprofen, aspirin, diclofenac, naproxen or similar painkillers.** They raise the risk of bleeding.
- **Do not take steroids or antibiotics** unless a doctor prescribes them.
- **Do not rely on papaya leaf juice or other home remedies** instead of medical care.
- **Do not think "fever gone means recovered".** The next 48 hours matter most.

## Stop Aedes mosquitoes

Aedes breed in small amounts of clean, still water, often in and around homes.

- **Every 3 days**, empty and scrub flower-pot trays, fridge and AC drip trays, buckets, drums and uncovered tanks.
- Throw away or cover old tyres, coconut shells, cups, packets, bottle caps and broken pots.
- Check rooftops, balconies, construction sites, basements and lift pits.
- Cover water containers tightly.
- Use nets, screens, repellent and long sleeves, especially early morning and late afternoon.
- Work with neighbours and the building committee. Mosquitoes fly between homes.
