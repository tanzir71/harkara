# CPR for children and babies

**Call 999 first if** a child or baby is unresponsive and not breathing normally. If you are alone with no phone, do 1 minute of CPR first, then go for help, carrying a baby with you.

Here a **baby** is under 1 year old. A **child** is from 1 year old until puberty. For teenagers who look like adults, use adult CPR.

Children usually stop breathing because of a breathing problem (choking, drowning, illness), so breaths matter more than for adults.

## Child (1 year to puberty)

1. Lay the child on their back on a firm surface. Tilt the head back and lift the chin.
2. If trained, give **5 rescue breaths**: pinch the nose, seal your mouth over theirs, blow steadily for 1 second, watch the chest rise.
3. Put the heel of one hand on the lower half of the breastbone. Use two hands for a bigger child.
4. Push down **one third of the chest depth** (about 5 cm), at **100 to 120 a minute**. Let the chest rise fully.
5. Give 30 compressions, then 2 breaths. Repeat.

## Baby (under 1 year)

1. Lay the baby on their back on a firm, flat surface such as a table or the floor.
2. Keep the head level, looking straight up. **Do not tilt a baby's head far back.** Lift the chin with one finger.
3. If trained, give **5 rescue breaths**: cover the baby's mouth **and** nose with your mouth, and puff gently, just enough to see the chest rise.
4. Put **two fingertips** in the centre of the chest, just below the nipple line. Or circle the chest with your hands and press with both thumbs.
5. Press down **one third of the chest depth** (about 4 cm), at **100 to 120 a minute**.
6. Give 30 compressions, then 2 breaths. Repeat.

If you cannot give breaths, do compressions only. It is far better than nothing.

## AED

- An AED can be used on children. Use child pads if there are any; otherwise use adult pads.
- On a small child, if the pads would touch, put one on the front of the chest and one on the back.

## Keep going until

Help arrives, the child starts breathing normally, or you are too exhausted to continue.

## Do not

- **Do not shake a baby or child** to wake them.
- **Do not hold a baby upside down** to start breathing.
- **Do not give anything by mouth.**

## Afterwards

Every child who needed CPR must go to hospital, even if they seem well.
