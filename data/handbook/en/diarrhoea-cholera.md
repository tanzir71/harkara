# Diarrhoea and cholera

**Go to hospital at once if** there is a lot of watery stool ("rice water"), the person cannot drink or vomits everything, is very sleepy or confused, has sunken eyes, passes little or no urine, has blood in the stool, or is a baby under 6 months. **Call 999** if they cannot stand or do not respond.

Diarrhoea means three or more loose or watery stools in a day. The danger is dehydration: the body loses water and salts. Cholera can cause severe dehydration within hours.

## Give ORS from the start

1. Use packet ORS mixed exactly as the packet says (most packets in Bangladesh: **one packet in half a litre, 500 ml**, of clean water). Or use the WHO home recipe: 1 litre of clean water, 6 level teaspoons of sugar and 1/2 level teaspoon of salt.
2. Give ORS **after every loose stool**:
   - Under 2 years: 50 to 100 ml (a quarter to half a cup).
   - 2 to 10 years: 100 to 200 ml (half to one cup).
   - Older children and adults: as much as they want.
3. Give small sips or spoonfuls every minute or two. If they vomit, wait 10 minutes, then go on more slowly.
4. Make fresh ORS every day.

## Zinc for children

Children with diarrhoea should take zinc for **10 to 14 days**, even after the diarrhoea stops (WHO advice):

- Under 6 months: 10 mg a day.
- 6 months and older: 20 mg a day.

Zinc shortens the illness and helps prevent the next one. Ask at a pharmacy or health centre.

## Keep feeding

- **Keep breastfeeding**, more often than usual.
- Give normal food in small, frequent meals: rice, khichuri, dal, banana, egg.
- Avoid soft drinks and very sweet drinks. They can make diarrhoea worse.

## Medicines

- **Do not give medicines that stop diarrhoea to children.**
- Antibiotics are needed only in some cases, such as cholera or blood in the stool. A doctor should decide.

## Prevent diarrhoea

- Drink only safe water: boiled, treated with purification tablets, or from a safe source.
- Wash hands with soap after the toilet, after cleaning a child, and before cooking or eating.
- Eat food hot and freshly cooked. Be careful with cut fruit, street drinks and ice.
- Keep flies off food, and use a covered latrine.
