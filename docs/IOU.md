# Harkara Khata — IOUs both people sign

*Banglish niche · English below*

**Ke tomake ei link pathiyeche?** Harkara app-e keu ekta baki-r hishab (IOU) likhe tomake mone koriye diyeche. Harkara-te IOU tokhon-i pakka hoy jokhon duijon-i sign kore. Tumi Harkara chalao na? Thik ache — taka dile bKash/Nagad TrxID take pathiye dao, se app-e "settled" kore debe. Notun IOU-r reminder-e ekta link thake (tanzir71.github.io/harkara/o/#c:…): seta khulle browser-ei IOU-ti mile dekhe confirm korte parbe, kono app ba account lagbe na.

**Someone sent you this link.** A Harkara user recorded an IOU with you and sent a reminder. In Harkara an IOU only counts once both people sign it; until then it is just their claim.

- **On Harkara?** Open the Khata tab. The IOU shows as *Needs your sign*. Sign it, or co-sign face to face by scanning each other's QR (works with no network).
- **Not on Harkara?** Nothing is needed from you. If you have paid, send them the bKash or Nagad TrxID (or tell them it was cash) and they can mark it settled. Reminders for newer IOUs carry a link to `https://tanzir71.github.io/harkara/o/#c:…`: open it to check the IOU's signature in your browser and **confirm** it with one tap — no app, account or phone number needed. Your confirmation is a short signed note with the one-time code from your link, sent to public relays.
- **Privacy.** IOUs travel end-to-end encrypted between the two people. Harkara never stores your phone number; the reminder SMS was sent from their own SMS app.
