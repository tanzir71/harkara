# Harkara Khata — IOUs both people sign

*Banglish niche · English below*

**Ke tomake ei link pathiyeche?** Harkara app-e keu ekta baki-r hishab (IOU) likhe tomake mone koriye diyeche. Harkara-te IOU tokhon-i pakka hoy jokhon duijon-i sign kore. Tumi Harkara chalao na? Thik ache — taka dile bKash/Nagad TrxID take pathiye dao, se app-e "settled" kore debe. Shighroi ei link khule browser-ei confirm kora jabe.

**Someone sent you this link.** A Harkara user recorded an IOU with you and sent a reminder. In Harkara an IOU only counts once both people sign it; until then it is just their claim.

- **On Harkara?** Open the Khata tab. The IOU shows as *Needs your sign*. Sign it, or co-sign face to face by scanning each other's QR (works with no network).
- **Not on Harkara?** Nothing is needed from you. If you have paid, send them the bKash or Nagad TrxID (or tell them it was cash) and they can mark it settled. A web page where you can confirm an IOU in your browser is coming soon.
- **Privacy.** IOUs travel end-to-end encrypted between the two people. Harkara never stores your phone number; the reminder SMS was sent from their own SMS app.
